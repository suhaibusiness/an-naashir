        Privacy

        In short: we know almost nothing about you, and we like it that way.

        No accounts, no cookies

        You need no account to look at or order a book. This site uses no cookies and no ads.

        Your basket stays on your phone

        The books in your basket are kept only in your phone's browser and are never sent to us.

        Orders go as a WhatsApp message

        When you order, your own WhatsApp opens with a ready message. You read it, change it if you like, and send it yourself. We only receive what you send, and use it only to deliver your books and reply to you. WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply there.

        No counting

        For now we do not even count how many people visit.

        Where the site comes from

        The site is served by a hosting company, which may keep standard technical records (such as your connection's address) for a short time for security. We do not use them to identify readers. Fonts, pictures and PDFs all come from this site itself.
